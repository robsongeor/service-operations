# Service Operations user guides

Welcome to the user guide for Service Operations.

These guides explain the everyday tasks available to your assigned role. The options you see may
be different from another person's because access is matched to the work each person performs.

## Why Service Operations was created

Service Operations is replacing disconnected spreadsheets and manual hand-offs with one controlled
record of each Job. It is intended to:

- reduce duplicate entry, missed Jobs and Job Number conflicts;
- keep the Job, Customer, Site, Contact and Equipment information connected;
- make it clear who created or changed a record and what still needs attention;
- automate reliable steps such as permanent Job Number allocation and TimeCloud entry creation;
- show GreenTree and TimeCloud status in the same place as the Job; and
- provide a consistent process that can be introduced to other branches and workflows over time.

The Auckland Job Book is the first spreadsheet process moving into the app. The rollout is staged so
the new process can be supported and improved before any other branch changes its existing process.

## Auckland Job Book migration

## Important rules

- Search before creating a Job, Customer, Site or Equipment record.
- Check every Job carefully before selecting **Add to Job Book**. A permanent Job Number cannot be
  deleted, reused or undone after it has been allocated.
- Do not create a second Job while the first save or TimeCloud upload is still pending.
- Most GreenTree Equipment is present, but some Equipment may still need its Customer and Site
  relationship set. Search before creating records and never duplicate existing Equipment.
- New Site addresses must be selected from the verified New Zealand address suggestions. Results can
  take a few seconds to appear; free-text manual addresses cannot be saved.
- Bulk Job creation and Site Check creation are not available in this release.
- TimeCloud creation is live in production mode. Its status is automatic and read-only.

## Need help?

Report problems as soon as possible. Include what you were trying to do, what happened and the
exact message shown on screen.

To include a screenshot on Windows, hold **Windows key + Shift + S**, select the affected area,
then paste the screenshot into your support email. Never include a password or verification code.
