# Service Operations user guides

Welcome to the user guide for Service Operations.

These guides explain the everyday tasks available to your assigned role. The options you see may
be different from another person's because access is matched to the work each person performs.

## Start here

- [Getting started and signing in](Getting-Started-and-Signing-In)
- [Job Book Admin guide](Job-Book-Admin-Guide)

## Job Book Admin at a glance

Job Book Admins can use:

- **Job Book** to find, add and correct entries;
- **Customers** to find Customers and Sites, and create them when needed; and
- **Equipment** to find machines, update approved machine details and move Equipment to the correct Site.

Job Book Admins do not assign or email technicians, change GreenTree or Timecloud entry markers,
review Job Cards, schedule operational work or use Service coordination.

## Need help?

If the app shows **Access has not been assigned**, the wrong menu options, or a repeated loading
error, record the wording shown on screen and contact the Service Operations administrator. Do not
create a second entry while an earlier save is still pending.
