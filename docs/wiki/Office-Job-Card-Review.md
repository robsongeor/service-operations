# Office Job Card review

This guide is for **Office Admins** processing technician Job Card submissions. Reviewing a Job Card
records the office workflow. It does not close the operational Job and does not send information to
GreenTree automatically.

Some recovery controls, including **Resume entry**, office corrections and **Technician returns**,
appear only when the shared Job Card service supports them. Their absence is a release capability
boundary, not proof that no recovery or missing-return case exists.

## Understand the queues

Open **Job Card reviews** and select the required stage:

| Stage | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Open jobs** | Numbered Jobs sent to technicians and still awaiting a Job Card. |
| **Submitted** | Job Cards received and ready for office processing. |
| **Needs follow-up** | Cards whose GreenTree entry has started or that need more information. |
| **Completed** | Cards whose office processing has a final recorded outcome. |

Use search and filters to find a Job Number, Customer, technician, Job type, attention item or office
administrator. Refresh before acting if another person may be working in the same queue.

## Review the submitted evidence

Open a card from **Submitted** and check:

- Job Number, Customer, Site, Equipment and technician;
- work performed and work still required;
- time, travel, parts and notes;
- safety and further-work flags;
- hour-meter reading, when supplied; and
- photos or other submitted evidence.

The technician's original submission is retained. Office notes and corrections do not rewrite that
original evidence.

Use **Save all documentation** when a complete offline record is required. The download can include
the completed Job Card PDF and original photos in one ZIP. Downloading does not complete the review.

## Start GreenTree entry

1. Select **Start GreenTree entry**.
2. Confirm the action.
3. Enter the card in GreenTree using the submitted evidence.

Starting records who began the work and when. It coordinates the shared queue but does not lock the
card, so always refresh and check the latest activity before completing it.

## Record follow-up

Select **Needs follow-up** when information or another action is required before GreenTree entry can
be completed. Enter a clear note explaining:

- what is missing or uncertain;
- who needs to respond; and
- what must happen next.

This saves an office note; it does not send a message automatically. When the issue is resolved and
**Resume entry** is available, select it. Earlier notes remain in Activity history.

Use **Record office correction** to append an audited explanation of an office correction and who
confirmed it. This does not alter the technician's original Job Card or update GreenTree.

## Mark office processing complete

Use **Mark complete** only after GreenTree entry is genuinely complete.

1. Recheck the submitted evidence and office notes.
2. Enter the optional GreenTree reference when available.
3. If hour-meter approval is offered, verify the reading and date. Confirm any correction or
   exception note requested by the form.
4. Confirm **Mark complete**.

This moves the card to **Completed** and records who completed it and when. It cannot be reopened in
this workflow. It does not close the operational Job or send data to GreenTree.

An approved meter reading updates Job usage evidence only when the app confirms that sync. It does
not complete the Job or update its service schedule. Use **Retry Job meter update** only when the
review shows that the meter update is pending or failed.

## Technician returns

When available, expand **Technician returns** to see whether each assigned technician's card is:

- Received;
- Awaiting card;
- Expired link — resend needed;
- Withdrawn; or
- No matching card sent.

Refresh the list before deciding that a submission is missing.

## Concurrent changes

If the app says another administrator changed the card, keep your draft open, select **Refresh
review**, read the saved change and then decide whether your action is still required. Do not try to
overwrite the other person's work.

## Report a problem

Include the Job Number, queue stage, action attempted, exact message and a screenshot. Hold
**Windows key + Shift + S**, select the affected area, then paste it into your support email.
